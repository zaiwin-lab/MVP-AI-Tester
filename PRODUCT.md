# PRODUCT.md — KAPT Digital Clinic

**register:** brand (public marketing gateway) + product (internal admin workspace)

## What it is

A Digital Solution Diagnostic Gateway for KAPT (the KOBIS AI Prodigy Team) at
KOBIS Berhad. Organisations describe an operational challenge in plain words;
KAPT returns a human-reviewed initial digital diagnosis within 24 hours. The
public side converts serious enquiries; the internal side manages the case
through to consultation, pilot, proposal, or project.

## Who it is for

Ministries, government agencies, local authorities, GLCs, statutory bodies,
cooperatives, universities, NGOs, associations, corporates, SMEs, training
providers, and event organisers — many of whom do not yet know what system they
need.

## Voice & positioning

Confident, measured, intelligent, practical, human. KAPT is a diagnostic and
solution-design partner, not a cheap software agency. Avoid hype words
(revolutionary, disruptive, world-class, transformation). Frameworks: AZUGAI7
(institutional depth), KODE5 (simplicity/usability), CONVAH7 (light — clarity,
trust, conversion; no aggressive scarcity).

## Design direction

Institutional, calm, trusted, mobile-first. Mood: "ministry seal under morning
light." Deep seal-teal primary (deliberately not generic corporate blue), warm
sand accent used sparingly, pure-white surfaces, dark institutional bands for
rhythm. Public Sans + Source Serif 4. No neon, heavy animation, futuristic AI
imagery, fake logos, or fake statistics.

## Non-negotiables

- The public user never gets an uncontrolled automated answer; humans review
  every response.
- No invented government partners, testimonials, logos, or figures.
- Privacy-first: warn against confidential/classified uploads everywhere.
- Keep the public experience simple; keep the internal workflow strong.
